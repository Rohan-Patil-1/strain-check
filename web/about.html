A small tool for a large question

Make invisible strain easier to see.

Strain Check turns ordinary phone video into an early, explainable ergonomic screening signal. It is designed to help people notice posture patterns, ask better questions, and decide what deserves a closer review.

SC
Author

Rohan Patil

Strain Check is an independent prototype built to explore privacy-conscious ergonomic assessment from everyday phone video. The project combines browser pose estimation, joint-angle measurement, and transparent RULA/REBA-inspired screening logic.

How it works

From a local clip to a useful signal.

01

Capture

Select a short work-task video from your device. The app does not force the camera open.

02

See the body

MediaPipe identifies body landmarks directly in the browser.

03

Measure

Landmarks become shoulder, elbow, knee, trunk, and neck angles.

04

Screen

Deterministic prototype rules produce RULA and REBA-style scores.

05

Review

Use the overlay, exported JSON, and annotated video to question the result.

Explainable rules

The score is built from visible measurements and thresholds rather than hidden training predictions.

Strain Check is a prototype screening tool, not medical advice and not a formal workplace assessment. Do not use it alone to make employment, legal, or safety decisions. Stop recording if an activity causes pain or feels unsafe, and obtain consent before recording another person.
