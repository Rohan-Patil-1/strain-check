Privacy

Your video stays here

The selected video is processed in this browser. This project does not upload, store, sell, or share your video with the author, managers, or a project server. Only the model files are downloaded.

The public CDN and hosting provider may receive ordinary request metadata such as IP address when loading website assets. The video file itself is not sent by this app.

Safety

Record safely

  • Use a light, normal object and never lift a dangerous load.
  • Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort.
  • Get consent before recording another person.
  • Do not use the result as a medical, legal, or workplace-safety decision by itself.
